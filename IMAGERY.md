# Imagery — read before launch

## The short version

**Every photograph on this site is a placeholder.** They are licensed stock
interiors, graded to the brand, standing in for Northern Edge Joinery's own
project photographs. They must be replaced with the client's own work before
the site goes live.

Nothing on the page claims these are Northern Edge projects. Captions describe
*the piece shown* ("Walk-in dressing room"), never a client, an address or a
job. The portfolio section points explicitly at Instagram as the real record of
the work. That framing is deliberate — swapping the files is all that is needed,
but until it happens the site must not be described as showing their portfolio.

## Why they are placeholders

The build had no access to the 97 photographs on
[@northern_edge_joinery_ltd](https://instagram.com/northern_edge_joinery_ltd).
Instagram does not permit automated download, and the account holder has not
supplied an export. Rather than ship an empty grid, the layout is filled with
consistently graded stand-ins so the client can see the finished design and
judge it.

## Licence

Source: [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com). The Pexels licence permits free
commercial use without attribution, and allows modification. The images were
downloaded, cropped and colour-graded; they are not redistributed as stock.

Using them beyond a design preview is still not recommended — a joiner's
portfolio showing other people's rooms is a commercial liability, not an asset.

## Replacing them

Drop the new files into `site/assets/img/` using the names below, then update
the `alt` text and the visible caption in the HTML to describe the real piece.

Run `python build/make_images.py` to regenerate the whole set from originals:
put full-resolution files in `build/hires/<id>.jpg`, update the `HERO`,
`SERVICES` and `PORTFOLIO` manifests at the bottom of that script, and re-run.
It applies the shared grade, crops to the right ratios and writes every WebP
and JPEG size.

## The grade

One grade is applied to everything so the page reads as a single shoot. It
lives in `build/make_images.py`:

```
saturation  0.86    colour pulled back so timber reads as tone, not hue
contrast    0.92    low-contrast, natural light
warm gain   R 1.045  G 1.005  B 0.955
black lift  +10/8/6 on a 0.82 range, lifting shadows toward warm ink
```

Photographs sit at `filter: brightness(0.82)` in CSS
(`--img-rest-brightness`) and animate to `brightness(1)` on hover, so the
grade stays restrained rather than baking the darkening in.

## Art direction, if new photographs are being commissioned

- **Finished rooms, not tools.** No sawdust close-ups, no gloved hands holding
  chisels, no workshop process shots. They undercut the positioning instantly.
- Warm, low-contrast, natural light. Shoot in the afternoon, not under
  worklights.
- Wide enough to show the piece meeting the room — the scribe against the
  chimney breast is the entire argument for buying bespoke.
- Portrait 4:5 for the grid, landscape 4:3 for the service blocks, 16:9 for
  the hero.
- Shoot at 2400px on the long edge or better.
